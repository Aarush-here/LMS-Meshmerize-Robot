**Current Ratings**

esp32 - 80mA

IR sensor array - 20mA

motors - 400mA (200mA per motor)

TB6612FNG Motor Driver - \~3mA

Buck Converter(5V) - \~12mA

**Calculations:**

total average system current = 515mA

battery capacity - 1500mAh



running hour = 1500/515 \~ 3 hours -> **2 hours** considering real life scenarios



Battery Chosen = 11.1V Li-Po battery

Li-po - less volatile , light weight , shorter charge , slight expensive

For these reasons , Li-po battery is chosen as weight is a dominating factor.

Source: https://robu.in/lithium-ion-battery-vs-li-po-battery/
