I have selected **TB6612FNG** over traditional L298N.

L298N requires huge heatsink as it experiences significant heat drop and significant voltage drop where TB6612FNG is efficient with minimal voltage loss.



Also,its maximum operating voltage is 15V > 11.1V LiPo battery so it does not need extra step-down converters for the motors. Also , it is smaller and lighter than L298N so it makes it ideal for techfest design.
